## Who gets the most value

The humanizer skill is ideal for content creators, marketers, and technical writers who need to ensure their AI-generated drafts sound natural and align with specific brand voices. It solves the pain of manually detecting and correcting AI-specific patterns in text, ensuring that final output reads smoothly and authentically.

## How it fits in a flow

Upstream: [Skill that feeds this] -> **This skill** -> [Skill this feeds]

The humanizer skill is typically invoked after an initial draft has been generated by another AI or writing tool. It acts as the final quality check before content is handed over to human reviewers for approval or published directly. For example, in a marketing blog pipeline, it follows the initial draft creation and precedes the review process.

## Measured outcomes

- **Source format vs our triad:** The skill operates differently from its source, integrating seamlessly into our workflow with specific configurations.
- **Voice-sample mode is the key differentiator:** Users can provide voice samples for more tailored humanization, resulting in higher satisfaction rates among clients and stakeholders.
- **Cycle compounds issue:** Running the humanizer on already processed text leads to over-correction. Best practice is to use it once per draft.
