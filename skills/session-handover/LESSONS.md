# Lessons — session-handover

## 2026-06-11 — initial release

- The single most valuable field is **next action**. Vague handovers ("continue where we left off") are useless; the next action must be specific and immediately executable.
- Always emit the session-opener block to chat in addition to writing HANDOVER.md. Users paste from chat at a much higher rate than they open the doc.
- Note uncommitted work in open items. A handover that says "task X done" when X is uncommitted in the working tree causes the next session to redo the work or wipe it.
- Capture the *reasoning* behind key decisions, not just the decisions. Future-you needs the "why" to know whether the decision still applies after new information.
