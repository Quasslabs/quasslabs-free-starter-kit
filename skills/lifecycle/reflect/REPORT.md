## Who gets the most value

Project managers and team leads who need to quickly capture and analyze project outcomes for continuous improvement. This skill solves the pain of manual retrospectives that are often delayed or biased.

## How it fits in a flow

Upstream: [skill that feeds this] -> **This skill** -> [skill this feeds]

The `reflect` skill is designed as a terminal step in various workflows, such as project management tools (e.g., Jira, Trello) and CI/CD pipelines. It integrates seamlessly into the end of any process where reflection on outcomes is necessary. For example, after completing a sprint or resolving a production incident, this skill automatically captures lessons learned and writes them to LESSONS.md in the project root.
