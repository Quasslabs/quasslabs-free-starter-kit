# Lessons — skill-builder

## 2026-06-11 — initial release

- Prior-art check is the highest-value step — most "new" skills are actually variants of an existing one. Always grep the registry first.
- Keep the header stub deterministic; let the human fill the body. Stub-only skills fail lint, which is the correct signal to finish drafting.
- The 7-file folder layout (SKILL/FUNCTIONS/LESSONS/INSTALL/SETUP/REPORT/manifest) is the minimum that satisfies `--strict-commercial`. Anything fewer fails the kit gate.
