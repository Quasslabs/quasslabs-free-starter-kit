# Other repos worth your time

Great work we can't bundle here — either because of its license or because it's a standalone tool, not a skill. Go straight to the source. (Several of the skills in this kit build on the ones marked ★.)

## The shoulders our skills stand on
| Repo | Why it's great |
|---|---|
| ★ [JuliusBrussee/caveman](https://github.com/JuliusBrussee/caveman) + [rtk-ai/rtk](https://github.com/rtk-ai/rtk) | The token-efficiency idea our `ug-ug` builds on. |
| ★ [mattpocock/skills](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills) (MIT) | The `tdd`, `zoom-out`, and `improve-codebase-architecture` lineage — excellent dev-agent skills. |
| ★ [garrytan/gstack](https://github.com/garrytan/gstack) | The GStack full-stack workflow our `gstack` skill builds on — great starting point for developers. |
| ★ [hardikpandya/stop-slop](https://github.com/hardikpandya/stop-slop) | The de-AI-ify-your-writing idea behind `humanizer`. |

## Bigger agent frameworks (when you outgrow a skills folder)
| Repo | Why it's great |
|---|---|
| [obra/superpowers](https://github.com/obra/superpowers) (MIT) | A full agentic **skill framework** in the same spirit as this kit — excellent reference for skill format + state-machine discipline. The closest "gstack-class" dev project. |
| [garrytan/gstack](https://github.com/garrytan/gstack) | Garry Tan's opinionated Claude Code tool roles (CEO, Designer, Eng Manager, QA…) — the workflow our `gstack` skill builds on. |
| [crewAIInc/crewAI](https://github.com/crewAIInc/crewai) (MIT) | Multi-agent role orchestration — when you need agents that coordinate, not just skills. |

## Build your own skill library (curated reading list)

| Repo | Why |
|---|---|
